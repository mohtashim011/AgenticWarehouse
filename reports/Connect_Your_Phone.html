Agentic Warehouse

Using your phone as a scanner

Your phone can scan stock in and out exactly like the computer at the bench. This takes about two minutes to set up, once.

What you need Connecting Scanning well If the camera stops Problems What to ask for

1 What you need

Three things, and nothing to install.

A phone with a camera Any reasonably modern Android phone or iPhone. You do not install an app — the warehouse system opens in the phone's own web browser.
The warehouse Wi-Fi The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer running the system. Mobile data will not work: the system is on your own network, not on the internet.
Your own login The same username and password you use on the computer. Every scan is recorded against the person who made it, so never use somebody else's.
Your stock data never leaves the building.

The phone talks directly to the warehouse computer over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing is sent to the internet, and the system keeps working if the internet goes down.

2 Connecting, step by step

You only do this once per phone. After that, save it as a bookmark.

1

Join the warehouse Wi-Fi

Open the phone's settings and connect to the same Wi-Fi network the warehouse computer uses. If you are not sure which one, ask your supervisor.

Turn mobile data off while you do this the first time. Some phones quietly prefer mobile data and then cannot find the warehouse computer.

2

Scan the square code on the computer screen

On the warehouse computer, open the page your supervisor points you at — it shows a large square code. Point your phone's normal camera app at it, and tap the link the phone offers.

If your phone will not read it, type the address in the phone's browser instead. It is written on the computer screen underneath the code, and looks like https://10.0.0.29:8443.

3

Accept the security warning — once

The phone will say the connection is not private, or that the certificate is not trusted. This is expected here and it is safe. Tap Advanced, then Continue or Proceed.

Why the warning appears, in one sentence.

That warning normally means "this website has not proved who it is to an outside authority". Your warehouse computer is on your own network and has no way to prove itself to an outside authority, so it signs its own certificate. You are being warned about a machine standing a few metres away from you. Accept it once and the phone remembers.

4

Sign in

Use your own username and password. If this is your first sign-in, the system will make you choose a new password before you can do anything — that is deliberate, because the password you were given is known to whoever gave it to you.

5

Open Scanner and allow the camera

Tap Scanner in the menu, then Start camera. The phone will ask for permission to use the camera. Tap Allow.

If you tap Block by mistake, the camera will never start. Fix it in the browser's site settings, or close the page and open it again.

6

Choose Stock IN or Stock OUT, then scan

The two large buttons at the top decide what a scan means. Check you are on the right one before you start — this is the single easiest mistake to make.

Hold a label in front of the camera. A small message appears in the corner saying what was counted and how many are now on hand, then clears itself so you can scan the next one.

Save it as a bookmark.

Add the page to your phone's home screen and you will never have to scan the square code again. On Android: menu, then Add to Home screen. On iPhone: the share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Write your warehouse address here
Your supervisor can read it off the computer. It changes if the router is replaced.

3 Getting a good read every time

Most failed scans are one of these four things.

Do this Why it matters
Use the back camera The front camera is lower quality and hard to aim. If the picture shows your own face, switch camera using the list under the viewfinder.
Hold the label level A barcode is read line by line across the bars, so a tilted label may never read at all. Square it up with the top of the screen. QR codes do not care about angle — that is why they are worth using where you can.
Fill the frame Move close enough that the label spans most of the width. Thin bars need pixels. If you cannot get closer, use the zoom slider.
Get light on it, not glare Use the torch button in poor light. Avoid shiny plastic wrap reflecting a ceiling lamp straight back — tilt the box slightly instead of the label.

Counting several of the same item

Holding one label in front of the camera counts one item, however long you hold it there. That is on purpose — otherwise a box left in front of the lens would add a dozen units a second.

To count five identical boxes, present each one separately, or take the label away and bring it back. If you are counting quickly and a scan does not seem to register, wait a moment: there is a short delay before the same code counts again.

4 If the camera stops working

Work does not stop. There are three other ways in, and the system moves between them for you.

1

It switches to the backup camera by itself

If the camera fails or simply stops reading anything, the system switches to another camera on that phone and tells you it has done so, and why. You do not have to do anything. If it cannot switch, it says that too.

2

Take a photo instead

Tap Scan a photo. A still photo is much sharper than a live preview, so this often reads a worn or curved label that the live camera cannot.

3

Type the number in

Tap Type a code and enter the number printed under the barcode. This always works, whatever the cameras are doing. It is never disabled.

If a code will not match anything

The system tries to repair damaged and misread codes automatically. If it is not certain, it shows you a short list of what it thinks the code might have been — pick the right one, or type it in. It will never guess silently and put stock on the wrong product.

5 When something goes wrong

Find what you are seeing in the left column.

What you see What to do
The page will not load at all Check the phone is on the warehouse Wi-Fi and not on mobile data or a guest network. Guest Wi-Fi deliberately hides devices from each other. If it still fails, the warehouse computer may be switched off — check with your supervisor.
"Your connection is not private" Expected. Tap Advanced, then continue. See step 3 above.
The page loads but the camera will not start Either you tapped Block when asked for permission, or the system was started without the secure setting. Try closing the page and opening it again first; if that does not help, tell your supervisor the server needs starting with --https.
"Another application is using it" Close anything else on the phone that uses the camera — a video call, the camera app itself — and press Start camera again.
The picture is live but nothing ever reads Check you are on the back camera, get closer so the label fills the width, hold it level, and try the torch. If it still reads nothing after a few seconds the system will offer another camera by itself.
"Already counted" Not an error. You presented the same label twice within a few seconds. Take it away and bring it back to count another one.
A scan was refused Read the message — it says why. Usually it is stock going OUT when there is none on hand. Nothing was changed.
You scanned the wrong direction Tap the message to open the scan, then Undo. The correction is recorded as its own entry; nothing is erased, and that is normal.
It worked yesterday, not today The address can change when the router restarts. Scan the square code again to get the current one, and replace your bookmark.

6 What to ask your supervisor for

If you are stuck, these are the exact words that will get it fixed fastest.

"What is the warehouse address for my phone?" They can read it off the computer at any time — it is shown on screen with the square code.
"Can you start the server with --https?" Say this if the page opens but the camera refuses to start. Phones will not allow camera access otherwise. It is a phone rule, not a setting anyone here chose.
"Can you allow Python through the firewall?" Say this if the page will not load at all even though you are on the right Wi-Fi.
"My login does not work." Accounts lock for ten minutes after five wrong attempts. Wait, or ask for an unlock and a password reset.
One last thing worth knowing.

Your phone is a full workstation, not a camera plugged into the computer. Anything you scan appears on the warehouse dashboard straight away, and anything a colleague scans appears on your phone. You are both writing to the same stock record.
