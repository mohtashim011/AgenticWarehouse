1.0x Zoom in until the barcode fills the width — thin bars need pixels.

Barcode not reading? Hold it level, not tilted, fill the frame, and use the zoom slider. Barcodes are read line by line, so a tilted label never decodes — QR codes are rotation-proof, which is why they scan at any angle. If a webcam still cannot focus on the thin bars, use Scan a photo: a phone photo is far sharper.

Laptop webcam works on localhost. Phone cameras require HTTPS (see the README for the tunnel option).
